Q3 2024 Performance Report
==========================

Executive Summary
-----------------
This report summarizes the financial performance and key operational metrics for Q3 2024.
Revenue increased by 18% year-over-year, driven by strong growth in enterprise subscriptions.

Key Highlights:
- Total Revenue: $4.2M (+18% YoY)
- New Customers: 142
- Churn Rate: 2.1%
- Net Promoter Score: 67

Product Updates
---------------
The platform received three major updates this quarter focused on performance and usability.
API response times improved by 35% following infrastructure upgrades in August.

Outlook
-------
Q4 pipeline looks strong with several enterprise contracts expected to close before year-end.
The team is on track to meet annual revenue targets of $16M.
